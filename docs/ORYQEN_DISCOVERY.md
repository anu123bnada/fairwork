## Identity direction

ORYQEN (OR-i-ken): open circular Q mark with an ascending terminal and a separate
point. Created as editable SVG and equivalent Android vector, with PNG exports.
Manrope is retained, loaded locally. Warm paper, deep evergreen, restrained mint;
opaque surfaces, clear borders, limited shadows and reduced-motion support.
Light/dark/device preference; private data is not cached in a service worker.
Manifest metadata does not certify offline support or installability on every OS.

The latest instruction supersedes exact legacy colors/glassmorphism for this brand
pass. Original Figma files remain historical references for hierarchy/journeys;
no approved ORYQEN Figma or motion specification was supplied. Do not label the old
Day 3–7 visual criteria passed or claim pixel-perfect parity with the new identity.
